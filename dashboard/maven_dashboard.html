Strategic Recommendations
1. Reduce SKU concentration risk — The Original Mr. Fuzzy drives 62.5% of revenue ($1.21M). A supply issue, quality defect, or trend shift could cripple the business. Target reducing this below 50% through active promotion of products 2–4 within 12 months.
2. Investigate Sugar Panda quality — 6.04% refund rate is the highest in the portfolio and 4.7× higher than the Mini Bear. Root-cause analysis is required. Fixing this could recover $13,000+ annually in lost refund costs and protect the 68.5% margin.
3. Formalize the Mr. Fuzzy + Mini Bear bundle — This pair was bought together 3,142 times organically — the strongest cross-sell signal in the data. A "Bundle & Save" offer at $69.99 (vs $79.98 individual) could boost AOV while increasing Mini Bear visibility.
4. Address Q1 seasonality — Revenue drops 35–40% every January–February vs Q4 peak. Valentine's Day (Feb) is an untapped opportunity for the Forever Love Bear. A targeted campaign could recover $50K+ in missed Q1 revenue.
5. Invest in the Hudson River Mini Bear — Best risk profile in the portfolio: 68.4% margin, 1.28% refund rate, $29.99 entry price. It's an ideal upsell and bundle partner but is under-promoted. Increasing its visibility could add $30K–$50K annually with minimal operational risk.
